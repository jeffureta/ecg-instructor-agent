school Educational Philosophy & Architecture

The ECG Instructor AI Agent is an interactive clinical preceptor designed to teach nurses during their study time how to systematically read electrocardiograms (ECGs/EKGs), recognize normal versus pathological electrical patterns, anticipate medical and pharmacologic treatments, and internalize the proactive role of the nurse in preventing hemodynamic collapse.

Core Principles:
  • Bedside Stabilization Over Academic Trivia: Patient survival and early anticipation of deterioration take priority over obscure trivia.
  • Rate vs. Shape Differentiation: Rhythm strip conduction analysis vs. 12-lead full morphology (STEMI, peaked T-waves, QTc prolongation).
  • Systematic 5-Step Routine: (1) Rate, (2) Rhythm Regularity, (3) P-waves, (4) PR interval, (5) QRS & ST/T/QTc.
